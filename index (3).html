How hard do you hit in Forever?

How the WoW Forever beta changes spell damage and healing compared with Classic. Read the spell breakdown, open the spell calculator, or pick your professions. Same rank, before talents, crit and buffs.

Forever cut base damage so gear carries it.

Mage and shadow priest spells need about 75 spell power to catch up with Classic. Warlock, shaman and balance druid need 250–700. Most heals need about 140 +healing.

How to read it

Spell power to match Classic
(Classic − Forever) ÷ coefficient

Forever at your spell power

What you actually hit for. The tables show 300 and 500; the calculator takes any value.

Example, Fireball rank 12: Classic 754, Forever 543, coefficient 100% → 211 spell power to match. At 500 spell power Forever hits for 1 043.

Fact check

All numbers checked against the sources on 23 Sep 2026.

Base values match

About 80 base values for mage, priest, warlock, druid and shaman match the ForeverChanges spellbooks.

Coefficients match

All 28 checked against 60.tools, e.g. Arcane Missiles 143% vs 120%, Bane of Doom 400% vs 100%.

Physical changes confirmed

Backstab +150 (was +225), Aimed Shot +166 (was +600), Raptor Strike +70 (was +140), Bloodthirst 35% AP + 48 (was 45%).

Players agree

Blizzard forum testers measured ~715 SP for Chain Lightning to break even; the data says 699.

Not DPS

Cast time, crit, mana, talents and rotation aren't included. A low number doesn't rank classes.

Plan professions for your group

Add the classes in your group or guild. Each player gets two professions so the group covers what matters most: rested XP for everyone, raid flasks, enchants and disenchanting, armor for every armor type, and each crafter with a gatherer feeding it.

Everyone should also take Cooking and First Aid; they don't use a profession slot. The plan fills the most useful roles first, so with a small group some roles stay open.

Where the steady income could be

No prices exist until launch, so this ranks by demand: what the datamined recipes show people will need over and over. Things that get used up every raid or every week sell more reliably than gear someone buys once.

Your group's camp

Each player near the campfire adds one camp object, and everyone gets the buffs for an hour. Pick what your group brings. One Camp Tent covers the whole group, so only one of you needs Leatherworking for the rested XP.

Campfire:

Rested XP from one Camp Tent covers everyone, so only one player needs Leatherworking. Camp buffs don't stack with the matching class buff (Incense Candle and Arcane Intellect, Lute and Mark of the Wild, Fish Bowl and Blessing of Kings, and so on), so skip objects your group's classes already cover. The skill 140 and 300 versions keep the same buff and add a utility, like a Repair Bot, a Greenhouse or a Molten Foundry.

Crafted tier sets

Datamined: each class set has six pieces. Legs, hands, shoulders and feet are crafted (skill 320, item level 61, level 60); head and chest aren't craftable. Legs cost 5 Malleable Essence of Nature, the other pieces 3. That essence comes from disenchanting raid loot. Datamined

Piece stats aren't shown in the client data yet. Tailoring makes cloth sets, Leatherworking makes leather and mail sets, Blacksmithing makes plate sets. Reagents: Tailoring uses Mooncloth, Bolt of Runecloth and Rune Thread; Leatherworking uses Cured Rugged Hide, Fel Leather and Rune Thread; Blacksmithing uses Arcanite Bar, Heavy Thorium Bar and Elemental Flux.
